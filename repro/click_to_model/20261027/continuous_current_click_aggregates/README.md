The stricter 5 px success rates are only 21.54%, 19.69% and 62.54% respectively. These are correct/all metric rates, not verified output coverage. Do not report only the favorable MSSD 0.1D threshold. `threshold_counts.csv` preserves all 336 rows: initialization, full tracking and the fixed BOP19 subset; ten MSSD thresholds, ten MSPD thresholds and one ADD threshold, each for working outputs and verified outputs. Threshold correctness is strict `<` for MSSD/MSPD and inclusive `<=` for ADD. Empty CSV fields mean null/not available; no selected verified rows means risk remains null.

Metrics use all legacy original-model vertex rows: bowl 8,323, wood 8,920, bleach 8,251. MSSD/MSPD minimize over the declared finite symmetry set (315 sampled bowl rotations, eight wood transformations, identity only for bleach). Continuous symmetry is not an exact continuous minimum, and absent declarations do not prove physical asymmetry. MSPD is normalized by 640/image_width; all three images are width 640. ADD preserves corresponding-point orientation and is symmetry-sensitive: bowl and wood have zero ADD<=0.1D tracking successes despite much better symmetry-aware results; bleach has 650/662. This discrepancy must remain visible, not treated as missing data or replaced by another metric.

`initialization_counterfactual.csv` contains only three already-sealed SAM-score alternatives to frame 0. They are actual previously recorded FP public returns, not derived replacement trajectories. Bowl MSSD improves 4.5651 → 4.3822 mm while MSPD worsens 4.0535 → 4.5760 px; wood MSSD worsens 7.0281 → 7.9735 mm while MSPD improves 6.3576 → 5.8094 px; bleach is byte-identical. Admission was not reassessed or upgraded. A future actual tracking comparison using the new SAM-score policy was not completed for this package. SAM scores predict mask quality, not pose confidence or instance identity; this mask-selection use is already described by the [official SAM2 predictor documentation](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2/blob/main/sam2/sam2_image_predictor.py).

The fixed independent numerical review recomputed 149 BOP tracking rows, all 3 actual initializations, EOAT indices 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 662, and 3 initialization alternatives using independent full-point formulas. Every one of the 4,893 rows' clocks, raw GT correspondence, validity and verified flags, plus all 336 threshold summaries, was checked. It did not recompute every tracking frame's geometry. Maximum MSSD difference was 5.48e-14 m and maximum MSPD difference 4.18e-11 px; sample threshold labels were strictly identical. Original raw rotations were retained without SVD repair or GT0 alignment.

Timing is instrumented: report tracking_seconds/FPS covers frame decode, shared measured motion, actual adaptive FP, CUDA synchronization and in-session evidence recording; final output serialization is excluded. Additional observation hooks write raw evidence. FPS is N_tracking/sum(recorded frame latencies), not an end-to-end or uninstrumented throughput claim. Initialization is reported separately and includes registration/admission/startup. No overhead was subtracted. Neural-iteration histograms are observed N-1 frame counts: bowl 2×2398 + 3×2, wood 2×1663 + 3×165, bleach 1×312 + 2×277 + 3×73. These are not evidence of budget savings without a controlled comparison.

Failure history is retained: original v1 failed all three children in the observer constructor before reading any of 4,893 inputs. V2's parent later exited 143 for an unknown reason. Bowl had completed normally; wood completed in its continuing child and was recovered without rerunning it; bleach was executed once under the unchanged plan. Wood's original parent wall time/returncode and the original combined batch wall time remain null. No reconstructed batch throughput is reported.

These are known development-set, raw-GT diagnostics, not held-out evidence, a causal study, or a BOP leaderboard score. No physical identity accuracy, deployment confidence, CVPR-level performance, VSD/ADI/AUC or new policy tracking benefit is claimed. The package contains aggregate numbers and source hashes only: no raw pose or GT matrices, images, masks, models, logs, source code or absolute local paths. The recorded historical source checks precede authorized subsequent development and do not freeze the evolving working checkout.

Files: `summary.json` gives whitelisted numbers/source hashes; `per_sequence.csv` gives three sequence summaries; `threshold_counts.csv` gives the entire 336-row grid; `initialization_counterfactual.csv` separates the three offline alternatives; `manifest.json` pins the other five files.
