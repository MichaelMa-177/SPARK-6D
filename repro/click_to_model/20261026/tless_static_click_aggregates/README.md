# T-LESS static click pilot: audited numerical aggregates

Across all **12 preselected click trials**, MSSD < 0.10 model diameter and MSPD < 5 normalized pixels each give **6 correct, 2 wrong and 4 unknown**. At the stricter MSSD < 0.05 diameter threshold, the result is **5 correct, 3 wrong and 4 unknown**. Independent numerical review passed for all four error metrics, all 40 threshold/population rows and all 6 click-pair records.

There are 10 qualified working pose outputs (10/12), 8 computable clicked-instance errors (8/12), and **0 admission-accepted outputs (0/12)**. Two object-2 clicks do not hit any visible GT mask of the declared object; their working poses remain unevaluated rather than being matched to the nearest prediction or another object. The two object-6 trials were unavailable because the fixed metadata selection bucket was empty. Neither unknown category is dropped or assigned zero error. At MSSD < 0.10 diameter, the two errors are object-18 trials A/B (MSSD 0.166187 m and 0.162641 m).

The 10 actual admission outcomes are 4 `assessment_timeout`, 2 `equivalence_budget_exhausted`, and 4 `ambiguous_supported_set`. The completed working poses are evaluated even when admission is unresolved. **Zero accepted outputs means selective risk is undefined (`null`), not zero error or successful validity checking.** This pilot still exposes the effective-admission coverage gap.

## Selection and scope

Six fixed metadata target slots (objects 1, 2, 5, 6, 18, 21) each have A/B single-click trials; five slots were available. Clicks were selected by the assistant from RGB images and sealed before GT access. A/B trials share the same image, so 12 clicks are not 12 independent scenes. This is a small development static pilot: **zero tracking frames**, no held-out generalization claim, no continuous physical-instance identity verification, no real-time claim, and no full BOP leaderboard score. The 9,072 planned candidate slots (7,560 captured and 1,512 unknown) are runtime completeness counts, not independent accuracy samples.

Posthoc instance association uses the original sealed pixel and all visible GT masks for the declared object in that image. Exactly one hit is required; no click movement, mask dilation, nearest-pose matching or posthoc candidate selection is used. Four available A/B pairs hit the same annotated instance; the object-2 and object-6 pair results are unknown. A static annotated-instance match does not establish identity persistence.

## Metrics and files

Evaluation uses every original vertex row of the independent BOP `models_eval` mesh. Vertices, GT translations, diameters and symmetry translations/offsets are converted from mm to m once. MSSD/MSPD use declared symmetry with action `GT @ S`. Objects 1/2 declare continuous symmetry; objects 5/6 declare discrete symmetry; objects 18/21 have none declared, which does not prove physical asymmetry. The continuous symmetry approximation uses 315 samples from fixed BOP discretization step 0.01; it is not an analytic continuous minimum.

Thresholds are strictly `<`: MSSD fractions 0.05 through 0.50 of diameter and MSPD 5 through 50 pixels normalized to width 640. The source images have width 720, so normalized MSPD = raw MSPD × 640/720; normalized 5 px corresponds to raw 5.625 px. ADD and `ADD_S_BOP_ADI_m` are diagnostics. The latter is the directed GT-to-estimated-surface nearest-neighbor mean, not a matching restricted to declared symmetry; it can hide orientation errors and cannot prove identity. No VSD or complete BOP dataset matching was run.

- `per_trial.csv`: all 12 trials, their output/association/admission states, metric values and geometry metadata. Empty numeric cells mean unavailable/unknown, never zero.
- `threshold_counts.csv`: 40 exact aggregate rows = 2 metrics × 10 thresholds × 2 populations. `accepted_only=False` keeps the full 12-slot population; `selected=10` is actual working-output coverage. `accepted_only=True` has an empty admitted population and null risk. `risk_known` uses only known labels, while `risk_lower` and `risk_upper_unknown_as_wrong` bracket unknown labels among selected outputs. The two unavailable trials still remain in the full denominator of every `correct_over_all` value.
- `summary.json`: the same 12 numeric records, all threshold rows, complete denominators, 6 pair statuses, scope limits and source SHA256 pins. The recorded GPU parent wall time of 432.657391272 s includes instrumented static initialization and process/journal overhead; it is not tracking throughput.
- `threshold_accuracy.png`, `.svg`, `.pdf`: identical aggregate plot in three formats; y-axis is correct trials out of all 12. The admitted-correct count of zero is not a zero-risk estimate.
- `manifest.json`: SHA256 and byte size of each payload file, excluding the manifest itself.

The official implementation is pinned to [BOP toolkit commit cea62d651c7e395b2e1962b9749e4e89693c6ac4](https://github.com/thodan/bop_toolkit/tree/cea62d651c7e395b2e1962b9749e4e89693c6ac4), including [pose errors](https://github.com/thodan/bop_toolkit/blob/cea62d651c7e395b2e1962b9749e4e89693c6ac4/bop_toolkit_lib/pose_error.py) and [symmetry discretization](https://github.com/thodan/bop_toolkit/blob/cea62d651c7e395b2e1962b9749e4e89693c6ac4/bop_toolkit_lib/misc.py). The independent reviewer recomputed errors without importing the producer or BOP evaluation functions; maximum absolute differences were 9.975e-18 m for MSSD and 7.283e-14 px for MSPD. Threshold and pair labels agreed exactly, and used-source/input hashes were unchanged. All receipt hashes are in `summary.json`.

This package contains only numerical derivatives, text and figures: no raw poses, GT poses/masks, images, meshes, weights, source code or absolute local paths. GT and visible masks were read only in the sealed posthoc evaluation and independent review after inference completed. Packaging only formats audited aggregates and does not recompute the metrics.
